# Platform Algorithm Best Practices

## Instagram (2024-2025)
- **Reels priority:** Instagram heavily boosts Reels; aim for 60% of output as Reels
- **Saves & Shares are king:** Algorithm weighs saves and shares far more than likes
- **Post frequency:** 4-5 times/week (feed + reels combined)
- **Stories:** 2-5 per day for staying top of mind without hurting feed rank
- **Best formats by engagement:** Reels > Carousels > Static images > Stories
- **Hashtag strategy (2025):** 3-8 highly relevant hashtags outperform 30 random ones
- **Golden window:** Respond to every comment in the first 60 minutes post-publication
- **Collab posts:** Use Instagram Collab feature to tap into collaborator's audience
- **Audio:** Use trending audio for Reels (within first 72 hours of trending)

## Facebook (2024-2025)
- **Video priority:** Native Facebook Videos (not YouTube links) get 5x more reach
- **Groups over Pages:** Facebook prioritizes group content in the feed
- **Meaningful interactions:** Long comments beat reactions; encourage discussion
- **Text posts:** Surprisingly still effective for community engagement
- **Live video:** Gets 6x more interactions than regular video on average
- **Post frequency:** 1-2 times/day for pages; 3-4/week for quality engagement

## YouTube (2024-2025)
- **Watch time + Click-Through Rate (CTR) = algorithm fuel**
- **Thumbnail CTR target:** 6-10% is strong; below 4% needs redesign
- **First 30 seconds:** Critical — hook immediately, no long intros
- **Chapters:** Add chapters to every video (boosts SEO + watch time)
- **YouTube Shorts:** Huge organic reach booster; use for cross-promotion
- **Community tab:** Post 2-3 times/week to stay in subscribers' feeds
- **Reply to comments:** Boosts algorithm ranking; reply within 24 hours
- **Playlists:** Group related videos; playlists drive 30% more watch time
- **Upload frequency:** 1-2 videos/week consistently beats bursts

## LinkedIn (2024-2025)
- **Golden window:** First hour of engagement determines post reach
- **Text-only posts:** Still outperform most formats for reach
- **PDF carousels:** Highest engagement and saves of all LinkedIn formats
- **No external links in post:** Put links in comments (algorithm penalizes external links in post)
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 relevant hashtags; avoid over-used ones
- **Best posting times:** 7-8 AM and 12 PM weekdays
- **Frequency:** 3-5 posts/week for optimal reach

## TikTok (2024-2025)
- **"For You Page" algorithm:** Driven by completion rate and shares
- **Hook in first 1-2 seconds:** Non-negotiable
- **Video length sweet spot:** 21-34 seconds for max completion rate
- **Trending sounds:** Using trending audio within 24 hours gives massive reach
- **Duet/Stitch:** High engagement mechanic; use for collaboration
- **Frequency:** 1-3 times/day for growth phase; 1/day for maintenance
- **Text overlays:** Add subtitles/text; 85% of videos watched without sound

## Blog / SEO (2024-2025)
- **E-E-A-T:** Google prioritizes Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness
- **AI-generated content:** Must be reviewed and human-edited for quality signals
- **Core Web Vitals:** LCP < 2.5s, FID < 100ms, CLS < 0.1
- **Helpful Content update:** Content must solve user intent, not just target keywords
- **Internal linking:** Minimum 3-5 internal links per post
- **Featured snippets:** Use question-answer format, tables, and numbered lists
- **Post length:** Long-form (2000+ words) still dominates for competitive keywords
- **Update old content:** Refreshing high-ranking posts boosts ranking further

## Twitter / X (2024-2025)
- **Threads:** Still the highest-performing format for reach
- **No external links:** Put links in replies to avoid reach penalty
- **Consistent posting:** 3-5 tweets/day for growth
- **Reply engagement:** Replying to large accounts' posts drives new followers
- **Twitter Spaces:** Live audio drives significant new follower growth
