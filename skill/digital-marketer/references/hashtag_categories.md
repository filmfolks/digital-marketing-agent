# Hashtag Categories & Industry Banks

Hashtag strategy formula:
- **2-3 Mega** (1M+ posts): Broad visibility
- **3-4 Large** (100K - 1M posts): Competitive niche reach
- **3-4 Medium** (10K - 100K posts): High conversion & community discovery
- **2-3 Micro/Brand** (<10K posts): Direct targeted audience & brand recognition

## Platform-Specific Rules & Guidelines

| Platform | Recommended Count | Placement | Best Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | 3 - 8 | End of caption or 1st comment | High relevance, avoid banned tags |
| **TikTok** | 3 - 5 | End of caption | Mix 1 trending, 2 niche, 1 specific |
| **LinkedIn** | 3 - 5 | Bottom of post | Professional tags only, no camelCase spaces |
| **Twitter / X** | 1 - 2 | Inline or at the end | Keep minimal to preserve character budget |
| **YouTube** | 3 (in desc/title) + 15 tags | Metadata tags field + description | Tags in video metadata matter for SEO |
