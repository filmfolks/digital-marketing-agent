# Copywriting Swipe File — 50+ Proven Hooks & CTAs

## 🎣 Instagram Hooks (First Line)
1. "Nobody talks about this, but..."
2. "The truth about [topic] they don't want you to know"
3. "I grew from 0 to [X] followers in [timeframe]. Here's what actually worked 👇"
4. "Stop scrolling. This will save you [hours/money/mistakes]."
5. "Hot take: [controversial opinion about your niche]"
6. "If you're still doing [old method], you're leaving money on the table."
7. "3 years ago I had [problem]. Today, [transformation]. Here's the exact steps 🧵"
8. "This one [tool/strategy/hack] changed everything for my business."
9. "Unpopular opinion: [your take]"
10. "The [role] who told me this was making $[X]/month. I didn't believe them until..."

## 📘 Facebook Post Openers
1. "Can I be honest with you for a second? [personal story]"
2. "Question for my [niche] community: How do you handle [problem]?"
3. "Last year I made a huge mistake with [topic]. Here's what I learned..."
4. "Real talk — [vulnerable statement]. Drop a 🙋 if you've been there."
5. "I've been in [niche] for [X] years and this is still the #1 mistake I see."

## 🎬 YouTube Video Hooks (Opening Line of Script)
1. "In the next [X] minutes, I'm going to show you exactly how to [outcome]."
2. "Most people do [thing] wrong. If you're one of them, don't worry — I was too."
3. "I spent [time/money] testing [topic]. Here's what I found."
4. "This is the [X]-step system I used to [achievement]."
5. "Before you do anything else today, watch this."

## 🧵 Twitter/X Thread Hooks

3. "[Number] lessons I learned the hard way about [topic]:"

## TikTok / Reels Opening Scripts
1. "POV: You finally figure out [problem]. Let me show you."
2. "Things I wish I knew before starting [topic]: number one..."
3. "Wait — you're still [doing old thing]? Try this instead."

## ✅ Call-To-Action Bank

### Engagement CTAs (Comments/Saves)
- "Save this post — you'll thank yourself later."
- "Tag someone who needs to see this 👇"
- "Comment your biggest challenge with [topic] below."
- "Drop a ❤️ if this resonated with you."
- "Share your take — agree or disagree?"
- "Which point hit home? Let me know in the comments."

### Follow/Subscribe CTAs
- "Follow for more [niche] tips every [day of week]."
- "Hit subscribe so you never miss a [topic] breakdown."
- "Turn on notifications — I post every [schedule]."

### Click/Traffic CTAs
- "Link in bio for the full [resource]."
- "DM me '[keyword]' and I'll send you the free [resource]."
- "Click the link below to [action]."
- "Get the full guide → [link/link in bio]."

### Sales CTAs
- "Limited spots — [CTA button] before [deadline]."
- "Join [number]+ [audience] who already [benefit]."
- "Start [outcome] today — first [offer detail]."
- "Book your free [service] call → link in bio."

## 📧 Email Subject Line Swipe File

### Curiosity
- "This changes everything about [topic]..."
- "Wait — did you see this?"
- "Weird question, but hear me out..."
- "The [niche] secret I've never shared publicly"

### Benefit
- "How to [desired outcome] in [timeframe]"
- "[Number] ways to [solve problem] starting today"
- "Your [outcome] cheat sheet is here"

### Urgency/Scarcity
- "Last call: [offer] ends at midnight"
- "Only [number] spots left"
- "This disappears in 24 hours"

### Personalization
- "[First Name], you're closer than you think"
- "We noticed you [action] — here's what's next"
- "Just for [segment], a special [offer]"

### Question
- "Are you making this [topic] mistake?"
- "What's your [metric] right now?"
- "Ready to [transform/achieve/start]?"
